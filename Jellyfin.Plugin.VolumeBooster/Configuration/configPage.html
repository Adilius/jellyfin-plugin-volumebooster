Lets Jellyfin play louder than 100% (no transcoding). Clients pick up changes after reloading (Ctrl+R) or restarting.

mpv volume is cubic, so (150% is about +10.6 dB).
Used by the volume keys, the mouse wheel and the extra shortcuts.
The on-screen indicator turns red at this level and above.
Separate several keys with spaces. Values are KeyboardEvent.key names, e.g. +, ], F9; keys typed with AltGr work. Avoid 0-9 (Jellyfin seeks with them). During playback the Up/Down arrows and the mouse wheel also go past 100%.
